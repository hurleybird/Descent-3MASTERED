# Dynamic shadows (experimental)

On the playable renderer branch, F2 → Video → **Dynamic shadows (per-pixel)** enables object-cast shadows. Per-pixel lighting must also be on. Shadows default off; the command-line overrides are `-dynamic-shadows` and `-no-dynamic-shadows`.

Version 1 picked a light by its distance from the eye, then put the room walls and the actors into the same map. In save 007 its chosen pulsing light made no visible difference at frame 50, even with the launcher's SSAA, MSAA, AO and bloom. The staged flare test wasn't representative. Putting room faces into their own shadow map also allowed their edges to be marked as blockers.

Version 2 ranks **light and caster pairs** by the potential projection of the object into the view. One slot is reserved for a live emitter (e.g. a flare). Four independent slots can represent real light-emitting room faces. Their locations, colors, areas and intensities come from level data. They are not additional additive lights. On lightmapped receivers we estimate the removable direct contribution using the game's own destructible-light radius; overlapping estimates share a bounded portion of the bake. Source separation is approximate: the original lightmap does not retain individual lights. The lightmap itself is never rewritten.

Only opaque, rigid model parts cast into this pass; nearby offscreen models and the player's actual exterior hull are eligible. Static room faces **receive but never cast** here, including for live emitters. Static architectural shadows remain baked. No mesh can shadow its own edges: casters and receivers are disjoint. There is no screen-edge detector, global darkening, flare displacement, change to the headlight, or change to AO. Empty shadow maps produce the original picture. The light views share a 384×384×30 R32F array (~17 MiB) and one depth buffer; only occupied directions are drawn. A stable disk filter uses blocker distance and lamp size for edge width.

Limits: authored lamps currently affect lightmapped surfaces in their own room. Live lights can shadow solid architecture and terrain; there is no outdoor sun pass, model-on-model shadowing, cutout-fence casting, or volumetric shadowing. A view without an illuminated receiver behind a qualifying caster legitimately shows no change. The headlight is still camera-aligned.

Gameplay repro: load mirror save 006, fire a flare, then turn without leaving the room. For the repeatable chase-camera comparison, use `-capture-frame 65 -capture-dt 0.016666667 -capture-seed 12345 -capture-fire-flare -capture-heading -capture-heading-from 12 -capture-chase-camera`. It uses the existing ship, level lighting, flare gun and chase camera. Compare with shadows off. To isolate the ship in cockpit view use `-capture-no-pilot-shadow`. To exercise the enabled receiver shader and all light selection while guaranteeing no blockers, use `-capture-shadow-clear-only`. These controls only operate during automated capture.
